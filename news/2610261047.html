Shadab's unbeaten century wins Pakistan a one-wicket thriller over England

Shadab Khan hit 113 not out and added a record 89 for the last wicket with Haris Rauf as Pakistan beat England by one wicket in Lahore to reach the Tri-Series final.

26 October 2026, 10:47

Pakistan pulled off a remarkable one-wicket victory over England at the Gaddafi Stadium in Lahore on Sunday, securing their place in the final of the ODI Tri-Series. They finished on 243 for 9 in 47.1 overs, chasing 242, with 17 balls to spare.

The win was built on an unbroken 89-run stand for the tenth wicket between Shadab Khan and Haris Rauf. It is the highest last-wicket partnership in a successful ODI chase. Shadab finished on 113 not out, while Rauf stayed with him to the end.

England, who were put in to bat after Pakistan won the toss and chose to field, were bowled out for 241 in 46.4 overs. Ben Duckett made 105 off 111 balls, with ten fours and two sixes, and Sam Curran added 42. Gus Atkinson contributed 36 late on. Captain Shaheen Shah Afridi was the pick of the Pakistan bowlers with 4 for 46.

Pakistan's reply got into trouble early. They were 83 for 4 after 15 overs, and the innings looked to be slipping away before Shadab and Rauf turned it around. Jamie Overton took 3 for 43 for England.

The defeat leaves England bottom of the standings after three straight losses in the tournament, and they now need a major turnaround to reach the final.

Pakistan launches PKR 16 billion Pasban scheme to reward formal remittances

The State Bank of Pakistan and the Pakistan Banks Association have launched a bank-funded rewards scheme with quarterly cash draws to steer remittances away from hundi and hawala.

6 November 2026, 10:00 · Source: State Bank of Pakistan, Pakistan Banks Association

ISLAMABAD — State Bank of Pakistan (SBP) Governor Jameel Ahmad has inaugurated the Pasban Remittance Reward Scheme, an industry-wide initiative that offers PKR 16 billion a year in cash prizes to encourage families to receive money from abroad through formal banking channels rather than illegal hundi and hawala networks.

The scheme was launched on 25 September 2026 under the auspices of the Pakistan Banks Association (PBA) and took effect on 1 October. It is funded entirely by the banking industry and places no burden on the national exchequer. According to PBA CEO and Secretary General Muneer Kamal, Pasban is the latest in a series of coordinated steps by the SBP, the government and the industry to support remittance inflows.

The scheme runs on the digital platform of payments network 1LINK, which tracks eligible inflows and generates draw entries automatically. Beneficiaries do not need to register, buy tickets or pay any fee.

Quarterly prizes

Each quarter, PKR 4 billion is distributed to 2,521 winners, with prizes credited to the bank accounts or digital wallets of the people receiving the money in Pakistan. The prize tiers are:

1st prize: PKR 100 million, 1 winner
2nd prize: PKR 25 million each, 20 winners
3rd prize: PKR 10 million each, 100 winners
4th prize: PKR 1 million each, 2,400 winners

How to qualify

A beneficiary account must receive home remittances worth at least USD 100, or the equivalent in another currency, in each of the three consecutive months of a quarter. Entries are calculated as digital tokens using SBP daily exchange rates: one token for USD 100 to 149, two for USD 150 to 249, three for USD 250 to 349 and four for USD 350 to 449, with one additional token for every further USD 100 and no upper limit.

Several categories do not qualify. Remittances collected as cash over the counter are excluded, as are inflows through Roshan Digital Accounts and any institutional, corporate or business accounts. Only personal single or joint accounts can win. Employees, officers and directors of the SBP, the PBA, 1LINK and the 27 participating banks are also barred.

Regional allocation

Prizes are allocated by region of origin. The Gulf Cooperation Council countries receive 50 per cent of each draw, the United Kingdom 15 per cent, Europe 15 per cent, North America 10 per cent and all other countries the remaining 10 per cent.

First draw

The first quarterly draw, covering remittances received in the final quarter of 2026, is scheduled for 15 January 2027. Results will be published on the 1LINK Pasban portal and through participating banks. Banks will contact winners directly and will never ask for personal credentials, PINs or financial transactions to release a prize.

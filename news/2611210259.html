Electric comb regrows nearly all lost hair in mice, beating Rogaine

Chinese engineers built a comb that sends treatment deep into hair follicles, restoring about 98% of hair on bald patches in mice within four weeks.

21 November 2026, 02:59 · Source: Nature Communications

An electric comb just went head to head with Rogaine and came out on top, regrowing nearly all the lost hair on bald patches in four weeks.

Engineers at Beihang University in Beijing built the device, which they named Detangle after Disney’s Tangled, and described it in Nature Communications this month.

Its teeth come in two lengths, with tiny electrodes in some and medicine-loaded tips in others, so each pass sends a gentle electric field deep into the hair follicles and helps two treatments slip inside the cells.

One is a gene that tells the scalp to build new blood vessels, and the other is a molecule that shuts down production of DHT, the hormone that shrinks follicles in pattern baldness.

In mice with hormone-driven hair loss, combing every three days restored about 98% of the hair on bald patches after 28 days. Daily minoxidil, the active ingredient in Rogaine, got to about 70%, finasteride reached about 50%, and the electric field on its own managed about 40%.

That gap matters because pattern baldness is the most common kind of hair loss in both men and women, and many people get only modest results from the drugs they have now, so a comb that drives treatment right into the follicle could make a real difference.

That said, the comb has only been tested on mice whose hair loss was triggered in the lab, and it would take human trials to show whether it’s safe for people and whether any new growth lasts. Even so, the team saw no skin damage in the animals, and they think the same approach could one day deliver treatments for other skin conditions too.

Study: Liu, F., Yao, F., Sun, H., et al. (2026). Combing-triggered electrostimulation synchronizing deep delivery for rapid hair regrowth in androgenetic alopecia. Nature Communications.

Australian Actor Richard Norton’s Death Triggered by Unapproved Peptide Injections, Coroner Rules

A Victorian coroner has ruled that actor and stuntman Richard Norton died from anaphylaxis and cardiac arrest after self-administering unapproved peptides.

30 October 2026, 17:29 · Source: Victorian Coroner’s findings

MELBOURNE — Veteran Australian actor, martial artist, and stunt coordinator Richard Norton died from a severe anaphylactic reaction and cardiac arrest caused by self-administered injections of unapproved peptides, a Victorian coroner has formally ruled.

The findings, released on October 28, 2026, by Coroner David Ryan, detail the circumstances surrounding the 75-year-old’s death on March 30, 2025, at the Alfred Hospital in Melbourne. The ruling marks what is believed to be Australia’s first documented fatality directly attributed to off-label peptide therapy.

Norton, celebrated for his performances alongside Jackie Chan and Chuck Norris, as well as his stunt and fight choreography work on major films including Mad Max: Fury Road and Furiosa, was prescribed a peptide combination of CJC-1295 and Ipamorelin to combat physical fatigue, age-related muscle loss, and joint pain.

Both CJC-1295 (a synthetic growth hormone-releasing hormone analogue) and Ipamorelin (a ghrelin receptor agonist) are non-TGA-approved experimental compounds. While widely promoted across anti-aging clinics and social media for recovery and muscle retention, they carry unquantified immunogenic risks when compounded and injected.

According to the coroner’s report:

  • Overdosage: Norton had been self-administering doses in excess of what was prescribed by his physician.
  • Delayed response to warning signs: Two days prior to his collapse, Norton informed a clinic nurse that he had been taking double the prescribed dosage. He was advised to step his dose down the following week rather than seek immediate emergency evaluation.
  • Fatal reaction: Hours after his next injection, Norton suffered an immediate, severe anaphylactic reaction, causing rapid circulatory collapse and cardiac arrest. Emergency responders were unable to revive him, and he died two days later.

The coroner highlighted systemic breakdowns in clinical oversight, noting that off-label prescriptions for experimental compounds by anti-aging practitioners often bypass standard safety evaluations.

Coroner Ryan has called on Australia’s Therapeutic Goods Administration (TGA) to tighten regulatory oversight surrounding compounding pharmacies, off-label peptide prescriptions, and wellness clinic sales models.
